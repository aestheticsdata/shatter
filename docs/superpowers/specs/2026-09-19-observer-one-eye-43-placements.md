# The Observer — one eye, 43 places

**Supersedes** `2026-09-18-observer-43-levels.md`, whose roster varied the eye's *anatomy* per level and was rejected on 2026-09-19: *"un oeil c'est un oeil, celui original était très bien."* The eye is the almond shipped in SHA-169, unchanged. What varies is **where it is, how big, on which layer, how visible, whether it moves, and what it does** — plus creatures on every level, and a boss at the end of each series (EVEN NINES: 9 / 18 / 27 / 36 / 43; EYE and THE WRATH slide 37/38 → 35/36).

The mockup's title — the eye enormous in the background — is the reference for "big in the background".

## The 43

**I — it learns to watch**

1. **SUNRISE** — it is the sun: huge, behind the wall, 20 % opacity, half under the horizon. Every brick broken, it rises a pixel; by the last brick it is up, awake, looking at you.
2. **SMILEY** — it lives in the smiley's left eye-bricks. Break the right eye's bricks and it jumps over.
3. **PYRAMID** — the eye on the dollar bill: tiny, at the apex. Dead stare until half the pyramid is gone, then it wakes and tracks.
4. **CHOMP** — deep in the throat between the jaws. Ball in the gap: it fires its gaze straight down it.
5. **GATEWAY** — patrols the sky between the two pillars, slow, left–right. First time it moves on its own. Ball through the gate: it stops and stares.
6. **HEART** — inside the heart, behind the bricks, and it beats: swells and shrinks on a pulse. Revealed as the heart dies.
7. **VORTEX** — the eye of the storm: tiny, dead centre, hidden inside the GGGG core until dug out.
8. **BOLT** — rides the lightning: starts at the top of the bolt, one stair down the diagonal every blink; at the bottom the screen flashes.
9. **THE VEIL** — boss, as shipped.

**II — it hides**

10. **CHECKER** — inside one brick. Break it and it moves to another. The eye-brick pays triple.
11. **INVADER** — the invader's eye, and it marches: steps sideways behind the wall, drops a row at the edge, peeks through a different gap each step.
12. **RAMPART** — a sentry: only its top half over the battlements. Ball near, it ducks and pops up further along.
13. **ROCKET** — the pilot in the porthole. Break the window: bigger, and out.
14. **HELIX** — climbs one rung a blink, up one strand, down the other.
15. **TETRA** — the next piece: appears at the top of the empty column, drops a row every few seconds, lands, respawns.
16. **ORBIT** — orbits on the zodiac ring's path: dim in the background at the top, solid and close at the bottom. A moon.
17. **COOL** — it is the first O. Erase it: the second O.
18. **THE IRIS** — boss.

**III — it wanders**

19. **HIVE** — the queen: huge, deep behind the comb at 20 %. Each broken cell brightens it.
20. **DNA** — runs the strands in a figure-eight, crossing at the middle.
21. **SERPENT** — the serpent's head: travels the snake path, along a row, down the gap, back along the next; glimpsed wherever a brick is missing.
22. **SKULL** — in the left eye socket, hollow-tinted. Clear the bone around it: the right socket.
23. **MIRROR** — the eye on the coloured side and its reflection at 30 % on the silver side, tracking the opposite way. Only the real one blinks.
24. **BUNKER** — through the pillbox slit: one thin strip visible, sliding behind the slot.
25. **CASCADE** — tumbles down the stairs one step per blink, fades at the bottom, reappears at the top.
26. **PLAY** — in the A's hole. Break the A and it plays: drifts round the field at 50 %, bouncing off the frame.
27. **THE TEAR** — boss.

**IV — it gets out**

28. **MAZE** — lost in the maze: walks the corridors one cell a second.
29. **OMEGA** — sits in the arch. Break the box and it descends through the omega's opening and leaves the field. First level it escapes.
30. **1991** — in a 9's loop, teleporting between the two 9s every blink.
31. **PILLARS** — walks behind the colonnade: strobes between the columns as it passes.
32. **GALAXY** — at the core, pulsing 30 % ↔ 100 %. A pulsar.
33. **SUPER MAZE** — at the one dead end the granite leads to, sensed at 20 % through the stone until the lasers reach it.
34. **PONG** — plays pong: rides the right edge tracking the ball's height; the ball bounces off it.
35. **EYE** — the level is its portrait: drawn behind the brick-eye at the brick-eye's size, the granite core is its pupil. Break the granite and the real pupil moves.
36. **THE WRATH** — boss (from 38).

**V — it comes for you**

37. **PACHINKO** — drops through the pins, bouncing at random, fades at the bottom, restarts.
38. **KEYHOLE** — someone at the door: huge behind the keyhole, only the part through the hole drawn.
39. **HOURGLASS** — it is the sand: top bulb, sinks through the neck to the bottom as the level empties.
40. **CENTIPEDE** — the centipede's head, crawling the body path, reversing at every silver.
41. **FLOPPY** — under the shutter: hit it, it slides open, the eye peeks, it slides shut.
42. **FINALE** — the mockup's title shot: enormous in the background at 20 %, doing every trick from the loop in turn.
43. **THE LID** — boss.

## The rule of 43 (2026-09-19)

**No two levels in the whole game share a disposition.** A disposition is the whole combination — place, size, layer, opacity, tint, behaviour — and two levels that both put the eye "in a brick" or both "behind the wall in a gap" are the same disposition however different the level. The list above has repeats to re-cut before series II: CHECKER / COOL / SKULL / 1991 (in bricks — SMILEY owns that), HIVE / FINALE (huge behind at 20 % — SUNRISE owns that), BUNKER / KEYHOLE / FLOPPY (through a window).

Series I as shipped in SHA-199: SUNRISE huge and faint behind the hills, rising · SMILEY in a brick pane, hopping · PYRAMID faint in the sky over the apex · CHOMP whole in the mouth's gap, behind the wall · GATEWAY half behind the left pillar · HEART hidden inside the heart · VORTEX small in the bottom-left corner, in front, 60 % · BOLT small at the bolt's tip, in front. The eye is blue on every placed level — red is a veil's state, and a red iris on BOLT was refused on sight.

## The shared tricks (the whole engine cost)

The almond, plus per socket: **opacity** (a dim tone set in colour, a dither in DEMAKE); a **layer** — background / behind the wall / inside a brick / clipped through a hole / in front; a **path** to move on — line, stairs, orbit, corridor, fall — with a speed and a trigger (blink, timer, bricks broken); a **hop** between named spots; a **pulse** (scale or opacity on a rhythm); a **duck** (hide when the ball is near, reappear elsewhere); an **exit** (leave the field). Eight things, reused across thirty-eight levels; nothing about the eye's face changes.

## The bestiary — twenty

The mockup carries only the three brood forms (its "comets" are lives). Seven exist on paper; thirteen are new. One line each: what it is · how it moves · what hitting it does.

**In the game (3)** — 1 **EGG** sits on the band · hatches. 2 **HATCHLING** walks, faster · takes wing. 3 **WYVERN** fast and wide · kill: a star.

**On paper, the chamber's four (4)** — 4 **PHOTON** a spark crossing in a straight line · bends the ball's path. 5 **ELECTRON** orbits one brick · takes its hit, a shield. 6 **NUCLEUS** a heavy blob · split: an extra ball. 7 **ANTIBALL** a black ring drifting · erases the ball it touches.

**New (13)** — 8 **MOTH** flutters round the eye · hit: drops the capsule it carries. 9 **SNAIL** crawls the top of the wall, its trail is mortar: a hit point back per brick passed · kill it early. 10 **WOODPECKER** perches and pecks a brick open in three, an ally · hit: flies off. 11 **SPIDER** drops from the ceiling on a thread when the ball passes, blocks it, climbs back · hit on the way down: thread snaps. 12 **CRAB** walks the band sideways, steals falling capsules · hit: drops them. 13 **BAT** asleep under the wall · a hit: five seconds of erratic flight, then hangs elsewhere. 14 **SLUG** slimes the rail: the deck skids there · pops, slime dries. 15 **FIREFLY** a point of light in the dark levels · hit: the field lit a second; kill all: stays dark. 16 **WISP** passes through bricks and the ball through it · laser only; pays the most. 17 **BEETLE** armoured; a hit rolls it into a charging ball; a second flips it three seconds, killable. 18 **JELLYFISH** floats up through gaps · reaching the rail stings the deck a second; pop it on the way. 19 **FROG** sits on a brick, jumps to another when the ball nears · hit mid-jump: bonus. 20 **VINE** grows from the band a segment at a time; reaching the wall it becomes a column · hit a segment: everything above falls.

Series I the gentle ones (egg, moth, snail, frog); the ally in II; the ones that touch the deck (slug, jellyfish, crab) in III–IV; V mixes everything. Every silhouette survives 1-bit.
